# Nehaarvi Classes Website Summary

## 1) Brief About the Business
Nehaarvi Classes is an educational institute focused on Mathematics and Accounting. The institute emphasizes concept clarity, board exam preparation, and confidence-building support for students.

## 2) Website Goals
- Build a clean, informative multipage website.
- Present the institute's performance and teaching strengths.
- Share subject details and contact information.
- Keep the site informational only (no registration or form).

## 3) Target Audience
- School students preparing for board exams.
- Parents seeking reliable coaching in Mathematics and Accounting.

## 4) Features Required
- Homepage with hero section and tagline.
- About page with results and institute profile.
- Programs/Subjects page.
- Contact page with phone and email details only.
- Responsive navigation bar.
- Interactive buttons/toggles for basic engagement.

## 5) Design Preferences
- Style: clean and professional, mid-level coding style.
- Branding colors:
  - `#4B2E2B`
  - `#8C5A3C`
  - `#FFDAB3`
  - `#7B542F`
- Include owner photograph as first visual on homepage.

## 6) Tools Used
- HTML (structure)
- CSS (styling and responsive layout)
- JavaScript (menu toggle and content interactions)
